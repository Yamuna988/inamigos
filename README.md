# Task 1 – NGO Research & Data Collection

## About the Task

As part of Task 1, I researched 10 NGOs working in India in different social sectors.

I collected basic information about each NGO, such as:

- NGO Name
- Website
- Area of Work
- Headquarters
- Key Initiatives

## NGOs Covered

1. Teach For India
2. Pratham Education Foundation
3. Goonj
4. SEWA
5. Akshaya Patra Foundation
6. HelpAge India
7. Wildlife SOS
8. Smile Foundation
9. Sightsavers India
10. CRY – Child Rights and You

## What I Learned

Through this task, I got an idea about how different NGOs work in areas like education, healthcare, women empowerment, child welfare, elderly care, and animal conservation.

I also learned how to collect information from websites and organize it properly in Excel.
